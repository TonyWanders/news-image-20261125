Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Sum up 25 November's world news in one image" bounty, $100 in USDC. See the bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125

A newspaper front page summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026 in five
stories. The program is `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library plus
Pillow): it draws the picture with `build_image.py` in a fixed layout
and writes the finished PNG straight to the target path. No network,
no clock, no random numbers, so two runs write byte-identical bytes.

## The 5 stories

1. **Houthis hit Riyadh's tallest tower** - Houthi fighters strike the
   tallest tower in the Saudi capital for the second time in under a
   week; Saudi Civil Defense says at least 11 people are wounded. The
   tower houses the Saudi Public Investment Fund headquarters.
   Source: Reuters World News Summary, 25 Nov 2026.
2. **Kyiv confirms ship strike** - President Zelenskiy confirms Ukraine
   hit a Russian cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland; ally Norway urges
   caution. Finnish, Estonian and Russian vessels joined the rescue.
   Source: Reuters World News Summary, 25 Nov 2026.
3. **CIA breach exposes data** - The CIA and the Office of the Director
   of National Intelligence find a cybersecurity breach that exposed
   some unclassified information, the New York Times reports. Classified
   systems were not affected. Source: Reuters, 24 Nov 2026 (NYT report).
4. **Huawei guilty on 10 counts** - A jury in Brooklyn convicts Huawei
   on 10 of 12 criminal counts, including bank fraud, wire fraud and
   conspiring to steal trade secrets, but acquits it of racketeering
   conspiracy and conspiracy to obstruct justice. Sentencing is set for
   14 September 2027. Sources: Reuters; Associated Press, 24 Nov 2026.
5. **Giuliani gets top honour** - President Trump awards Rudy Giuliani
   the Medal of Freedom, the highest civilian honour in the US.
   Source: Reuters World News Summary, 25 Nov 2026.

Also today: Sarkozy corruption case to trial; WHO chief urges the US to
lift its oil blockade on Cuba; 25 people held hostage in a Cartagena
supermarket; Sweden's parliament to vote on Kristersson staying PM.
